HackerTube data
===============

Source: https://hackertube.org/data
Dataset exported 2026-10-31: 6,454 videos, 39,834 labeled Hacker News comments,
150 topic categories in 16 areas, and the related-video graph.

Licensed under ODC-By 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

HackerTube was prompted into existence by Andrej Karpathy and built by Claude
working in Claude Code. Video data comes from the YouTube Data API and comments
from the public Hacker News archive. Video titles and channel names belong to
their creators.

This application is an independent project and is not affiliated with
hackertube.org, Andrej Karpathy, Hacker News, Y Combinator, YouTube or Google.
