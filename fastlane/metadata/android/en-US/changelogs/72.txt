HackerTube 32.1 is the first public HackerTube release for Android.

- Bundled the complete HackerTube catalogue: 6,454 videos across 150 topics, plus 27 curated playlists.
- Added HackerTube-only home rows, browse pages, related videos and autoplay.
- Added site filters for year, content type, duration, hidden gems, evergreen status and sorting.
- Added the Longs tab for every video over one hour.
- Added mixed search across videos, subjects and curated playlists.
- Added locally stored Liked and Watch later lists.
- Removed YouTube channel, comment and recommendation surfaces from catalogue playback.
