<i>HackerTube</i> brings the videos Hacker News keeps recommending to each other to Android. It gathers talks, lectures, documentaries, explainers and deep dives from nearly two decades of Hacker News discussions into one browsable catalogue.
<br />
The complete catalogue is bundled with the app: over 6,000 videos organised by 150 topics, plus 27 curated playlists. Browse with filters for date, content type, duration, hidden gems and evergreen videos, or open Longs to find every video over an hour.
<br />
Search understands subjects as well as video titles, and related videos and autoplay stay inside HackerTube. Like videos and save them to Watch later locally on your device.
<br />
<i>HackerTube</i> is an independent project and is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Hacker News or Y Combinator.
